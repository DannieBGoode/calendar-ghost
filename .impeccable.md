## Design Context

This context covers the **landing page** in `site/` (calendarghost.com). The application's own
context lives in `PRODUCT.md` and `DESIGN.md`; the landing page follows the Landing Page Register
Rule in `DESIGN.md`.

### Users

Members of self-hosting communities (r/selfhosted, Hacker News, selfh.st, homelab forums) who
arrive from a shared link, often skeptical, on desktop or phone. They want to know in seconds what
the app does, whether they own their data, and how hard it is to run. Some use screen readers,
reduced motion, or browse with JavaScript off.

### Brand Personality

Playful, honest, capable. The ghost is the main character: it carries events between calendars,
reacts, and jokes in microcopy. Explanations stay plain and literal (short active sentences, true
to the `CONTEXT.md` glossary). Confident about maturity ("use at your own risk", never "pre-alpha").
Emotional goal: "this is delightful, and these people know what they are doing."

### Aesthetic Direction

- Must feel amazing, fun, and dynamic, with moving pieces; it currently reads as inferior to the
  references.
- References: keeper.sh (clear copy, first-class self-hosting), calendarpipe.com (hero shows the
  product transforming a real event), agentic.littleplains.com (animation is the argument: pick an
  input, watch the system work, see the output).
- Brand assets to keep: the ghost mark, the Twilight palette (Lantern Indigo), light and dark
  following the device. Typography may evolve if the current pairing holds the page back.
- Anti-references: generic SaaS template (identical card grids, gradient text, glows), terminal
  cosplay, anything that looks AI-generated.
- Show the product through focused, cropped UI moments (the rules list, a health hero), not
  full-page screenshots with app chrome.

### Design Principles

1. The animation is the explanation: every moving piece teaches what the app does.
2. Privacy is shown, not claimed: the "Busy" ghost crossing over is the core image.
3. Honest and specific copy; nothing the product does not do.
4. No trackers, no third-party requests, works without JavaScript, respects reduced motion.
5. One memorable idea per section; no filler sections.
