# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is the administrator of a self-hosted installation, commonly running on a Raspberry Pi. The interface must guide a nontechnical calendar user through account connection, rule setup, preview, incident recovery, and routine health checks while keeping audit and provider diagnostics available progressively for technically comfortable operators.

## Product Purpose

Calendar Ghost keeps selected calendars synchronized through explicit one-way rules without requiring a developer-operated service. Success means the administrator can understand what is synchronized, preview privacy consequences before enabling a rule, trust that destination projections match their sources, and recover safely when authorization or provider access fails.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, precise. The product should feel like a quiet household utility: clear when healthy, specific when attention is required, and never promotional or theatrical.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic SaaS analytics dashboard with oversized metric cards, dense decorative charts, promotional copy, or a permanent icon-heavy sidebar. Do not imitate Google Calendar, and do not turn routine setup or incident recovery into an infrastructure console or terminal-like experience.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with current health and the next meaningful action.
2. Explain synchronization in calendar language, exposing provider diagnostics only on demand.
3. Make privacy and destructive effects visible before a rule can write anything.
4. Preserve evidence through previews, event mappings, reconciliation reports, and audit history.
5. Keep routine operation quiet; interrupt the administrator only when action is genuinely required.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard operation, visible focus, screen-reader labels and live status announcements, non-color health indicators, sufficient text and control contrast, reduced motion, and responsive layouts down to mobile width.
